Why live vitals change triage

A static intake form cannot hear a murmur or catch falling oxygen. Streaming the M1 audio and vitals into one dashboard lets the AI flag the patients who need a clinician now — and lets the clinician trust the flag, because the trace behind it is right there on screen.
