KHOY × Signhify StudioConcept sample — 3D sofa-bed viewer

One sofa. Two modes.

Drag to rotate. Flip the sofa into a bed — then see how it ships: vacuum-packed into a single cardboard box.

Fabric: Sage Sofa · seats 3 · 200 × 90 cm

KHOY sofa beds are designed to unfold at home and ship vacuum-packed in cardboard — KHOY says this cuts delivery costs by up to 95% versus a traditional sofa. The transform and box shown here are a concept visualisation, not KHOY’s own product imagery.
